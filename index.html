About me

I grew up in a small, loving and supportive family originally from Iran, which has played a major role in shaping who I have become today. A quote that I live by everyday is this too shall pass unknown that comes from ancient Persia. It always reminds me that no matter what you’re going through, if you’re in the good days of life or the bad that all will pass. I have been so in love with this quote that for my 18 th birthday I got a tattoo to always remind me that everything is temporary!

Growing up I have always dreamed big and had a passion for creativity and art, which lead me to choosing theIMD program. I hope to turn my passions into skill and knowledge and dream of becoming the artist of my dreams which can express herself freely and turn the pains and gains of life into art!
